# Company Intranet – Complete User Guide

*A plain‑language walk‑through of every module, page, button and flow.*

This document explains **what each part of the intranet is for, what you see on the screen, what every button does, and how work flows from one page to the next**. It is written for everyday users and managers, not for developers. Technical details (databases, APIs, code) are deliberately left out.

## 1. Getting started

### Login
- Open the intranet address in your browser. You see the sign‑in screen with the tagline *"Sales, deliveries and stock in one place."*
- Enter your **Work email** and **Password**, click **Sign in**.
- If you forget your password, an administrator resets it in **Admin settings › Users** (or **User Management**).

### The navigation bar (left side / top)
The menu is grouped so you can find things quickly:

| Group | Items |
|---|---|
| **Workspace** | Home, Calendar, Documents, Paperless, Project boards |
| **Operations** | Sales, Logistics, Stock, Tenders |
| **Company projects** | Condoms, Sanitary pads, HBA1C |
| **People and money** | People, Finance |
| **Oversight** | Management, Flows |
| **Support** | Support tickets |
| **Tools (⋯ menu)** | Company news, Docs, To‑do, Requests, Uploader, PDF tools, Books, Email archives, Desktop notifier |
| **Top‑right** | Messages (unread badge), Notifications bell, Profile & password, Appearance (Light/Dark, Comfortable/Compact, "Same as this computer"), Admin settings, Sign out |

**You only see the modules you have been given access to.** Module access and finer permissions are set per user by an administrator (see section 29).

### Things that appear everywhere
- **Welly** – the company AI assistant. A chat bubble floats at the bottom‑right of every page; many pages also have "Welly suggests", "Ask Welly" or "Analyze with Welly" buttons.
- **PIN / password prompts** – some sensitive actions (importing sales, uploading PODs, requesting deliveries, opening the Asset Register, department boards) ask for a short PIN or password before continuing. This stops accidental or unauthorised changes.
- **Row menus (⋮)** – most tables have a three‑dot menu at the end of each row with the actions for that record.
- **Filters & "Clear filters"** – lists have search boxes and drop‑down filters; a *Clear the filters* button resets them.
- **Export Excel / Download PDF** – most lists can be exported.

## 2. Home

**Purpose:** your personal landing page – a dashboard you can customise.

**What you see**
- **Waiting for you** – approvals/tasks assigned to you (Urgent/High are highlighted). Click **Review** to open them.
- **Deliveries today / Overdue loads** – quick logistics health; **See loads** jumps to Logistics.
- **Meetings today** – with a **Calendar** link.
- **Your tasks due this week** – split into *Overdue* and *Today*.
- **Flow widgets** – Active runs, Waiting on people, Completed, Failed or cancelled, Tasks for me.
- **Business widgets** – e.g. *Sales by company*, *Stock by warehouse*.
- **Shortcuts** – your own quick links to pages or actions.

**Buttons**
- **Customise / Edit** – turns on edit mode. Then:
  - **Add widget** → *Customise Home* dialog: pick from the **widget library** (ready‑made by flow designers), choose a **Flow** (or "All flows"), what to **Show**, **Period** (Last 7/14/30 days), optional **Title**, then **Add to Home**.
  - **Add a shortcut** → choose a *Page or action* and a *Label*, **Save**.
  - **Reset** restores the default layout. **Done** leaves edit mode.

## 3. Company news

**Purpose:** the company notice board.

- Shows **Announcements** (New Company Announcement, Birthday Today, Upcoming Birthday, New Employee, Employee of the Month, Support Ticket Resolved, Welcome to the Team!).
- **View All Announcements** opens the full list; **Back to all announcements** returns.
- Announcements are created by admins in **Admin settings › Announcements** (title, message, priority Low/Normal/High/Urgent, category General/Important/HR/IT/Finance/Operations, target audience All users / Managers only / Admins only, optional expiry).

## 4. Calendar & To‑do

### Calendar
- Month view of **Events & Tasks**, **Birthdays This Month**, **ToDo Tasks** and **Events**.
- South African public holidays are built in (Human Rights Day, Good Friday, Family Day, Freedom Day, Youth Day, Heritage Day, Day of Reconciliation, Christmas Day, Day of Goodwill).
- Event types: Team Meeting, Project Deadline, Company Holiday, Training Session, Client Presentation.
- Click a task to **Mark Complete**; **Got it** dismisses a reminder.

### To‑do (tools menu)
- Shows **Pending** and **Total Tasks**, tasks assigned to you and tasks you assigned to others.
- **Create Task / Assign a Task** → *Task Title, Description, Due Date, Priority (Low/Normal/High/Urgent), Assign To, Category (Work, Meeting, Follow‑up, Project, Personal)*.
- Notifications panel: **Mark all as read**.

## 5. Documents, Department Hub & Shared with me

### Documents (Portal)
- Landing page listing each **department's document hub** plus **Shared with Me**.
- **Access Hub** opens a department hub (some hubs require a department password).

### Department Hub (one per department)
A simple file manager:
- Toolbar: **Upload Files** (or drag & drop "Drop files here to upload"), **New Folder** (asks for *Folder Name* → **Create**), **Select All / Deselect All**, **Refresh**, **Move**, **Delete**.
- Per file: **Open**, **Preview**, **Download** (some types show *Download to View*), **Rename** (*New Name*), **Move to…** (pick *Destination Folder* → **Move Here**), **Share**.
- **Share** dialog: share with a whole **department** or search a **person by name or email**, add an optional **Note**.

### Shared with me
- Everything other people have shared with you. **Open** the file or **Remove share**.

## 6. Docs (collaborative documents & spreadsheets)

**Purpose:** write documents and spreadsheets together, in the browser, with Welly's help.

**Docs list** – **New document**, **New spreadsheet**, **Import** (Word/PDF/scan), **Open**, **Settings** (title, description, share with people), **Delete**.

**Document editor** – works like a word processor:
- **File** menu: Import Word Document / Import PDF / Import Scanned Document; Export as Word (.doc) / HTML; **Email Document**; **Print**.
- **Edit**: Undo, Redo, Cut/Copy/Paste, Select All, Find (Ctrl+F), Replace (Ctrl+H).
- **View**: Fullscreen, Zoom In/Out, ¶ marks, Navigation, Page colour, Margins (Narrow/Normal/Wide), Page size (A4/Letter/Legal).
- Formatting: fonts, sizes, indent/outdent, UPPER/lower/Title case, Table of Contents, Footnote, Link, Quote, Code.
- **Comments** side panel (**Post**, resolve).
- **Share** – gives others edit or view rights. The header shows *Editing – make changes* or *Viewing – read only*.
- **Ask Welly** panel: Fix Grammar & Spelling, Summarize, Rewrite Professionally, Improve Writing, Translate (Afrikaans, Zulu, Xhosa, Sotho, Tswana, French, Portuguese), Generate Content → **Apply to Document** or **Insert at Cursor**.

**Spreadsheet editor** – **Import**, **Download**, **Save**; standard cell editing.

## 7. Paperless

**Purpose:** a document workflow area – view a PDF, annotate it, get it **signed** and **approved**, and share it. Approval decisions coming from Finance, Management and Flows end up here.

- **Document viewer** with tools: annotate, draw, sign (draw a signature or upload one), stack pages.
- **Workflow panel**: signers and approvers, *sequential* or *parallel* order. Buttons **Mark signed**, **Mark approved**, **Mark viewed**; **Expand workflow**.
- Document categories: Invoice, Purchase Order, Delivery Note, Statement, Payment Schedule, Recon, Proof of Delivery, Tripsheet and more. *Select a category to continue* is required before saving.
- **Share & notify** – emails the people on the workflow (shows *Email sent*, *Shared, but email failed*, or *Sharing failed*).
- **Rename document**, **Remove document**, add an **annotation/Note** with optional **Reason**.

## 9. Sales

### Header
- **Welly suggestions** – opens Welly's fulfilment advice (see below).
- **More ▸ Import sales** – asks for a 4‑digit PIN, then the **Import Sales Report** dialog: *Select Company*, optional *Report Period*, drop the Excel file → **Upload & Preview** (shows KPIs, a duplicate warning and an issues list) → **Commit Transactions** or **Cancel Import**.
- **Request delivery** – password → *Request Urgent Delivery* form: tick invoices, Priority (Normal/Urgent), Qty, Description, Address, Notes → **Submit**. The request lands in **Logistics › Incoming Requests**.

### Tabs
1. **Incoming orders**
   - *Company Performance* cards → click for a **Company detail** dialog (Sales trend, **Export Excel**, **Generate PDF**, **Compare**).
   - KPIs: Total Customers, Invoices, Monthly Revenue, Order Emails Today.
   - Order e‑mail list (filters, **Refresh IMAP**, mailbox PIN). Row menu: **View details**, **Copy sender**, **Create order**, **Forward to logistics**.
2. **Customers**
   - Search, Province filter, **Import customers** (PIN).
   - Row: **Edit**, **View Invoices**, **Geocode Address**, **Create order**, **View history**, **Delete**. A details side‑panel opens on click.
   - **Data Issues** dialog: *Customers Without Locations* (**Ask Welly to Fix**, **Geocode All**, **Auto‑Geocode**, **Enter Address**) and *Invoices Without Province* (**Ask Welly**, **Bulk assign**).
3. **Invoices**
   - Search, Status filter, **Request delivery**.
   - Stock chips per invoice: *In stock / Partial / No stock / Unmapped*.
   - Statuses: **Pending** = not on a trip sheet yet; **Assigned** = on a trip sheet; **Delivered** = load delivered / POD received.
   - Actions: **Edit**, **Request Delivery**, **Print**, **Email**, **Duplicate**, **Delete**.
4. **Sales history** – company chips, search, date range, *Products only* toggle, **Reconcile** (shows matched/missing banner); expand an invoice to see lines.
5. **Back orders** – search; **Unmapped products** dialog (**Create** or **Link** to a stock item); stock availability bar; stats; company sub‑tabs.
6. **PODs** – filters Region / Month / Driver / Link status; Grid or List; **Welly scan unlinked** → **Apply links**; **Upload POD** (password); per POD **View / Download**, **Welly scan this POD**, **Link/Unlink trip sheet**, **Link/Unlink invoice**, **Edit POD**.
7. **Trip sheets** – Search, **Refresh**, **Import trip sheet**, **Create trip sheet**; **View** opens the sheet with **Sign / Approve / Reject / Delete**.
8. **Tender pricing** – lookup by Tender No., search, company chips, **Export Excel** (prices include 15% VAT).

### Welly suggestions dialog
Warehouse chips at the top; tabs **Fulfil now**, **Internal movements** (**Send all to Logistics** / **Send**), **Inter‑company transfers**; panels *Decision flow*, *Steps*, *Assumptions*; **Re‑run**.

## 11. Logistics

**Purpose:** plan loads, run the fleet, keep drivers/vehicles/fuel/maintenance in order, and collect proof of delivery.

### Header
- **Barcode lookup** box – type/scan a code to find a load or shipment.
- **Live map**, **Scan and track**, **Client bookings** buttons.
- Sub‑navigation: **Loads · Fleet · Movement · Maintenance · Trip sheets · Overberg Tripsheets · PODs · Fuel history · Requisitions · Controller · Reports**.

### 11.1 Loads
KPI tiles (each one is clickable):
| Tile | What clicking does |
|---|---|
| **Active Loads** | filters the list |
| **Address Issues** | opens *Address Issues* dialog – customers with bad/missing addresses. **Welly's Suggested Fixes** → Select All / Deselect All → **Apply Fixes**, or fix City/Province/Postal code/Lat/Long manually → **Done** |
| **Puma Garage · open** | opens the Puma Garage fuel orders dialog (see below) |
| **TFN Orders** | TruckFuelNet fuel orders (warning if more than 5 open) |
| **Available Vehicles / Drivers** | Vehicles / Drivers management dialogs |
| **Incoming Requests** | delivery requests from Sales/Company projects (filters: status Pending/Approved/In Transit/Delivered/Cancelled, Department) |
| **TFN Depots** | map of fuel depots (filter by province, "Near trip route") |

Loads table: Load #, Date, Customer, Route, Vehicle, Driver, Status (shows **LIVE · Track** when the truck is out today). Row menu: **View Details** (Overview, Stops, Items, Timeline, Attachments, **Print TripSheet**, Route map with *planned vs actual*), **Copy link**, **View Route**, **Track**, **Update Status** (Assigned → … → *Completed (POD received)*), **Reassign** (driver/vehicle), **Reschedule** (new pickup/delivery date + reason), **Reassign Province**.

**Create Load** – Customer, Origin Warehouse, Vehicle, Driver, Scheduled Date, Priority (Low/Normal/High/Urgent), one or more **Stops** (Delivery Address, Company/Location, Contact, Phone, Notes), **Calculate** route, General Notes → **Create Load**.

**Daily Dispatch Report** – pick a date → provincial breakdown & vehicle dispatch details → **Export Excel**.

### 11.2 Fleet
- Counters: **Linked / Unlinked** (to CarTrack GPS), search box.
- **Sync CarTrack**, **Sync TFN**, **Refresh**, **Add Vehicle** (Registration, Make, Model, Year, Colour, VIN, Vehicle Type, Tank size, Fuel type Diesel/Petrol/LPG, Status Available/In Use/Inactive/Sold/Decommissioned, Branch GP/KZN/CPT/PE).
- Vehicle cards show CarTrack/TFN badges, driver, location, speed state (MOVING / IDLING / STATIONARY / OFFLINE), tank size and TFN balance.
- Card menu: **View Details**, **Edit Vehicle**, **Assign Driver**, **Move to branch**, **Link/Unlink CarTrack**, **Link/Unlink TFN**, **Track Location**, **Fuel History** (fill‑ups, distance between fills, *Fuel Health Score*, irregularities), **Maintenance History**, **Movement History** (today / date range, km average, harsh events), **Remove vehicle (sold/scrapped)**.
- **Live Camera Streaming** (CarTrack Vision) where fitted.

**Vehicles dialog** – filter by Status/Type; **Edit Vehicle**, **Assign Driver**, **Move to Maintenance**, **Mark Available**, **Delete Vehicle**.
**Drivers dialog** – stats (Total Drivers, Available, Trips Delivered, KM Driven); **Add Driver / Edit Driver** (names, nationality, passport, licence number/type B/C1/EC, licence expiry, DOB, phone, hire date).

### 11.3 Movement
Two views:
- **Fleet movements** – GPS trips per vehicle: filters From/To, Province, search "Vehicle, driver or place", quick ranges Today / Yesterday / 7 days / This month / 30 days; **Export**.
- **Internal movements** – stock moving between warehouses. Status chips (Open & recent, Requested, Planned, Assigned, In transit, Received, Cancelled); **New internal movement** (From/To warehouse, add stock lines, optional vehicle/driver, planned date, priority, notes → **Create movement trip**). Flow buttons: **Accept & assign → Dispatch → Receive** (*Receive into stock* with reason). Lines not linked to a stock item block dispatch until fixed.

### 11.4 Maintenance
- Stat cards: Overdue, In Progress, Scheduled, Licenses Expiring, Completed.
- Filters: Search, Vehicle, Status, Type (Scheduled Service, Repair, Spares, License, Inspection, Other), Assigned To, Date range (1 m / 3 m / 7 m / All time).
- **Schedule Maintenance** (type, priority Low→Critical, target date, date stood, estimated cost, assigned to, licence expiry) · **Export to Excel** · **Refresh**.
- **License Expiry Alerts** panel → **Schedule License Renewal**.
- Row actions: **Start**, **Complete** (odometer, actual cost, notes, attachments → **Mark Complete**), **Edit**, **Change Status**, **Upload Files** (Maintenance Files panel, drag & drop up to 50 MB, Download/Delete), **Delete**.

### 11.5 Trip sheets
- **Create Trip Sheet** → choose a type:
  - **Import Tripsheet** (Excel) · **DN Tripsheet** (delivery notes) · **Manual Tripsheet** · **Internal Transfer** (warehouse to warehouse) · **AI Powered Tripsheets** (pick warehouse & vehicle, Welly groups invoices into loads) · **Overberg Tripsheets**.
  - The wizard: pick invoices (**Available Invoices**, part‑delivery option) → **Route Configuration** (pickup warehouse, stops, **Match from Customer DB**, geocode, optimise, distance matrix) → **Trip Sheet Summary** → **Preview PDF** → Create.
  - **Welly's Tripsheet Wizard**: drop Excel → **Analyze with Welly** → select warehouse → check addresses → driver & vehicle → review → **Create Tripsheet**.
- Filters: search, **Warehouse** toggles (ALL/GP/CPT/KZN/PE), **Status** (Draft, Pending, Assigned, Scheduled, Active, In Transit, Completed, Cancelled), date range; active filter chips with **Clear all**.
- Table: Trip #, Load #, Driver, Vehicle, Route, Warehouse, Stops, Distance, Est. time, Date, Status, Created by.
- Row menu: **View Details**, **Copy link**, **View Picking Slip**, **View Route**, **Edit**, **Assign / Reassign Driver‑Vehicle**, **Reschedule**, **Print**, **Download PDF**, **Email Trip Sheet + Picking Slip** (to warehouse manager), **Continue Draft**, **Generate Load** (turns the sheet into an active load), **Delete**.
- **Imported Invoices** panel (Pending / Assigned / Delivered / Queries; company & province filters) with **Suggested Trips** (AI) and **Create Tripsheet** from selected invoices, **Add to Load**.

### 11.6 Overberg Tripsheets
For the Overberg courier partner: **Waybills awaiting confirmation → Confirmed, not on a tripsheet → On tripsheets → On the road → Delivered today → Exceptions**. Buttons **Accept / Decline (all)**, **Tripsheet**, **Send**; **Portal logins** tab to create partner logins (**New portal login**, **Email new temporary password**, **Edit name / phone**).

### 11.7 PODs (Proof of Delivery)
- **Delivered without POD** panel – delivered loads with no POD (count, unbillable value, oldest age). Filter > 7 / 14 / 30 days; **Upload POD** per load.
- Filters: Search, Region, Month, Driver, Link Status; **Upload POD**; **Reconcile → Tripsheets** (matches PODs to trip sheets by invoice number and closes trip sheets where every invoice has a POD); Grid / List toggle.
- Stat chips: Total PODs, Linked, Tripsheets, Invoices, Not Linked, per region, Drivers.
- POD card/row: **View**, **Download**, menu **Link to Tripsheet / Delink**, **Link to Invoice(s) / Delink**, **Edit Details**. Click the trip number to preview the trip sheet, click invoice numbers to see details.
- **Upload POD** dialog: Driver name, Delivery date, Region (GP/KZN), notes; Welly reads the document and suggests invoice numbers (**Add invoice number**, **Re‑scan**, **Skip**, **Link Invoice**).

### 11.8 Fuel history
- Period picker; stats (Vehicles, Transactions, Litres, Rand, R/L).
- Views **Summary / Transactions / By Group**; filters by registration, group, **Fuel Source** (TFN or Puma Garage).
- **Export Excel** (custom date range), **Sync from TFN**.

**Puma Garage dialog** (from the Loads KPI): **New Order** (vehicle & driver pickers; link icons show whether the vehicle/driver matched a fleet record), **Import Orders** (paste from the recon sheet), table with status *Open → Slip in → Closed*. **Upload slip** (photo of the filled slip), **Close** (only allowed once a slip is uploaded), **Reopen**, **Delete**, **Show all**.

### 11.9 Requisitions
Driver cash/e‑wallet requisitions and sleep‑out requests.
- Period, search, status (Active/Cancelled). Table: RF #, Paid To, Cell, E‑Wallet, Reason, Diesel, Actions.
- **New Requisition** (RF Number, Paid To, Cell, E‑Wallet amount, Diesel amount, Payment method E‑Wallet/Bank Transfer/Cash, Reason) · **Print requisition**.
- **New Sleep Out Request** (Select tripsheet, Driver, Date, Amount, Notes). Requisitions go to **Finance › Approvals** for payment.

### 11.10 Controller
Live control‑room map: **Show all vehicles**, **Fuel Stations**, **Active Loads** routes, **Reload Vehicles**, **Add Zone** (Parking / Depot / Restricted / Customer, radius, region), trips per day with **Export Excel**, **Copy URL**.

### 11.11 Reports
- **Daily Incident Reports** – pick a date, **Refresh**, **Export Excel**, **Show all incidents**.
- **Suspicious Fuel Health Ratings** – choose *Days to analyze* → findings and fill breakdown.

### Live map / Scan & track / Client bookings
- **Open Map – Live Fleet**: all vehicles, fuel stations, active loads, customers; **Open in new tab**.
- **Scan & Track**: **Start camera** to scan a QR/barcode, **Look up**, **Register this code**, **Mark loaded**, **Create tag & QR**, **Print label**.
- **Client bookings & shipments**: queue (Awaiting acceptance, On the road, Due today, Delivered today, Open exceptions); **New booking** (lines, shipments, Google driving estimate, **Check**, **Create booking**); **Accept / Reject**, **Plan onto load**, **Re‑plan**, **Mark in transit**, **Waybill**, **Labels**, **Tracking link**, **POD PDF**.

## 12. Stock Management

**Purpose:** know what is on hand in each warehouse, keep a movement ledger, approve dispatches and run stock takes.

**Header:** **Welly suggests**; warehouse cards (click to filter every tab; 👁 opens the 3D view); company chips.

### Tabs
1. **Stock on hand** – KPIs; **Search / Refresh / Export / Transfer / Adjust / Receive Stock**. Shows up to 400 rows; codes in green are on invoices.
2. **Stock counts** – **Export**, **Apply Invoice Prices** (PIN), **Count Template**, **Import Count** (PIN), **New Stock Take** → enter counted quantities, see variance, give a reason (required), **Post N adjustments**.
3. **Issues** – Open / In Progress / Resolved; **New Issue**; **Start working → Resolve / Dismiss / Reopen**.
4. **Exceptions** – Out of Stock, Negative, Unpriced with Stock; **Export**.
5. **Movements ledger** – alert if delivered loads were never deducted (**Review in Dispatch**); **Link stock lines** (PIN); **Record a movement** (Receive, Return, Transfer, Adjust, Write off); **Undo** reverses a movement; last 500 rows.
6. **Dispatch** – sources Tripsheets / Picking Slips / Invoices; status Awaiting approval / Deducted / Already counted. **Approve Dispatch** dialog: link unmapped lines, set Dispatch Qty, reason if more than on‑hand → **Approve & Deduct**.
7. **Reports** – Valuation by Company, Movement Activity, Dispatch Compliance, Exceptions; **Export Excel**.

### 3D warehouse
Building selector, **Search Inventory**, **Filter by Status**, Move/Lock, **Reset Camera**, **Fit to Content**, click a box for details and **Transfer to Building** → **Confirm Transfer**. Legend: Active / Low Stock / Empty / Blocked.

**Import Stock on Hand** (Admin › Data import or Uploader): select company, drop Excel, snapshot date → **Upload & Preview** → **Commit Records**.

**Tender detail** – tabs *Overview, Key Dates, Workflow Status*; **Upload Document**, **Add Line Item** (BOQ: description, quantity, unit cost, unit price – shows *Below cost!* warning), **Assign Team Member**, **Mark Complete**. **Ask Welly**: *Analyze Documents & BOQ*, *Extract BOQ from Documents* (**Import Items to BOQ**), *Quick BOQ Analysis*, *Pricing Review*, *Compliance Check*, **Copy Analysis**.

**Artwork Vault** – labels, packaging, inserts, brochures, logos. **Upload Artwork**, **Link Artwork to Tender**, **Send to Marketing / Graphics** (change request with recipients, priority, requested‑by date).

## 15. People (Human Resources)

### People dashboard

## 31. Common rules & conventions

- **Invoice life‑cycle:** Imported (Sales) → *Pending* → put on a **trip sheet** (*Assigned*) → trip sheet **generates a load** → load **delivered** → **POD** uploaded & linked → Finance **Book Keeping** shows *Has POD* → paid/allocated via **Remittances**. A delivered load **without a POD is unbillable** and is flagged in Logistics › PODs and Finance.
- **Stock life‑cycle:** Receive → on hand → Dispatch approval deducts → Movements ledger records it; stock takes post adjustments; **Undo** reverses a movement rather than deleting it.
- **Money out:** Books *Request for Payment*, Logistics *Requisitions*, Stationery requests and Finance Request dialogs all land in **Finance › Approvals**, where they are *Marked paid* or *Sent back with a reason*.
- **Warehouses / branches:** GP (Gauteng), KZN (Durban), CPT (Cape Town), PE (Gqeberha).
- **Companies:** Promed Technologies (PMT), Access Medical (ACM), Promed Pharmatech (PHT), Sebenzani Trading (SBT).
- **Colours:** green = linked/OK, amber = needs attention/missing link, red = overdue/error.
- **Nothing is final until you confirm.** Preview/Analyse steps never change data; only *Commit*, *Approve*, *Post*, *Apply*, *Save* do.
